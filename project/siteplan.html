QMS Consultancy: Website Plan

Site Name

QMS Consultancy

The name states the service directly: Quality Management System (QMS) consulting. It is short, easy to remember, and tells visitors right away what the business does.

Optional domain idea: qmsconsultancy.com

Site Purpose

The site guides small and medium-sized businesses in setting up and organizing a quality management system. It provides information on QMS basics and standards, an overview of consulting services, and a contact form for requesting a consultation.

Scenarios

  • What is a quality management system, and does my small business really need one?
  • What steps are involved in getting my business ready for ISO 9001 certification?
  • How much time and money does it usually take to set up a QMS?
  • How can I contact a consultant to book a free consultation?

Color Scheme

The palette pairs a trustworthy, professional blue with a warm red-orange accent. All combinations were chosen for good text contrast.

Color Hex Code Where It Is Used
Blue #1F4E8C Header background, headings, links
Red-Orange #C2410C Buttons, accent borders, hover states
Light Blue-White #F5F8FC Page background
Dark Gray #222222 Paragraph text

Typography

The site uses a single font family, Roboto, in two weights. It is clean, highly readable on screens, and works well for a professional consulting brand.

Element Font Weight Sample
Headings (h1, h2, h3) Roboto, sans-serif Bold (700) Quality Made Simple
Body text and navigation Roboto, sans-serif Regular (400) We help your business build a QMS that works.

Wireframes

Home page layout in mobile and desktop views. Both show a header with logo and navigation, a hero banner with a call-to-action, three service cards, a short "Why QMS?" section, and a footer with contact details.
